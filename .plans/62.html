#62 · Standup mode: displaced swimlane bands and clipped column headers on narrow windows

cattanisimone/gh-reloaded#62 · Size: normal (layout fix in Standup's own CSS and content script, new fixture variants, geometry e2e) · Goal: at 1500 / 1100 / 800px each lane band sits at the top of its lane and sticks under the column-header row, and each compact column header is one clean row whose pill is never cut.

What changes for users

  • In Standup mode the assignee (lane) band stays at the top of its lane at every window width: no more band sitting on the first card and no empty white strip under the column headers.
  • While scrolling, each band sticks right under the column-header row and is pushed away by the next one, without overlapping. It spans every column, also after scrolling sideways.
  • Compact column headers are one row at every width: the title ends in “…” first, the Effort: 19 pill (or the count) always stays whole, and the grey scrollbar under the title is gone.
  • The gaps between bar, header row, bands and cards are the same at 800px as at 1500px. Exit, Esc and navigating away still restore the page as today.

Root causes (reproduced)

SymptomCauseEvidence
1. Band displaced over the cards, empty strip above itGitHub writes an inline top on each lane band (styledGroupHeaderWrapper__…, position: sticky) equal to the column-header row's height, measured before Standup compacts that row. The row then gets shorter, the band keeps the old offset and is pushed down by the difference, leaving its own slot empty. On 0.11.2 the uncompacted headers wrapped letter by letter at 1100 / 800px (fixed for Full-width by #59), so the row was very tall when GitHub measured it: hence the 130 / 190px offsets in the issue and the near-correct 1500px.Live public roadmap board with the extension loaded: header row 46px after compaction, band inline top: 58px → band 12px low at all widths (Full-width on). Fixture: 16px low.
1b. Same, on boards where a lane scrolls sideways on its ownA lane wrapper with overflow-x: auto (computes overflow-y: auto too) becomes the band's sticky scroller. The lane never scrolls vertically, so top only pushes the band down inside its lane, and the band can never stick under the header row.Fixture ?lanes=scroll: band 52px below the lane top at every width. The issue's hypothesis; not seen on the public board (one scroller), designed for anyway.
2. Pill cut, grey scrollbar in the headerCSS specificity: [data-ghsm-row] [data-ghsm-keep] { flex: none } (0,3,1) beats [data-ghsm-row] h2 { flex: 0 1 auto } (0,2,2). The heading is a kept part, so it never shrinks and pushes the pill out. The header frame (ColumnFrame__…) is overflow-x: auto on the live page, so the overflow shows as a scrollbar.Fixture with an Effort: 19 pill: frames overflow and the pill is cut at 1100 and 800px, with Full-width on and off; fine at 1500px.

Risks and open questions

  • GitHub may re-measure and rewrite the band's inline top later. Default: our !important rule wins over the inline value regardless, and we re-measure on resize and re-render.
  • Flattening per-lane scrollers could change what Full-width board's lane-scroll mode detects. Default: Full-width re-measures on its own observer and simply sees no per-lane scroller while presenting; covered by the Full-width-on runs of each test.
  • The real field-sum pill markup on a private board is not in any fixture. Default: model it as a second AggregateLabels__ counter next to the count (the shape the compaction code already recognises) and ask the maintainer to look at it live.

Out of scope

  • Bar text vs first column left edge (#59 alignment, already merged in #61) and dependency arrows over bands and pills (#58).
  • Follow-ups seen live, not fixed here: the lane band's collapse chevron and ⋯ menu are not hidden (on current markup horizontalGroupHeader__ is the column-header row, not the lane band, so compactGroupHeader targets the wrong element); the bar's summary note shows a horizontal scrollbar when it does not fit.
