Size: normal · PR #107 · Issue #105 · Goal: replace the layout-card design, which needed a fix in every review round, with one that meets every constraint by construction.

What this re-plan changes and what it keeps. Kept as approved at 6a5f33e and confirmed by the last two reviews: the Triage ▾ button (anchor on Group by, designed fallback after Sort by, Primer look, 1012px switch), the preferences contract (prefs.js, ghntModules, ghntNativeInbox, ghntLayout), the route gating, the state matrix, the thumbnails, permissions (none). New approach: a fixed card grid with a text budget

  • Panel 440px (was 380; the mockup panel is about 455), min(440px, 100vw − 32px). Cards become 132px instead of 112px, and the title and description no longer share a row with the radio.
  • One card box model, fixed rows: thumbnail · title (2 lines reserved, 36px) · description (1 line, nowrap) · marker line (18px, “✓ Default” only on Integrated, empty elsewhere). Heights are equal by construction, and nothing needs to measure itself.
  • The radio is visually hidden, not removed. A native <input type="radio"> per card keeps the radiogroup, arrow keys and screen reader semantics (C9). The visible indicator is a 2px accent border, an accent tint and a ✓ badge in the thumbnail corner (C1). The focus ring is drawn on the card with .ghnt-card:has(input:focus-visible).
  • Panel-local text reset: every text class in the panel sets its own font-size, font-weight, line-height and color, so GitHub’s label, h2 or p rules cannot leak in (C5).
  • Copy limited by a budget: every description must fit on one line in the widest font available to the tests with at least 6px to spare, and every title word must fit on a line. New descriptions (measured in DejaVu Sans 12px, which is wider than GitHub’s stack): In the sidebar 83px, Filter pills 58px, Collapsible list 87px. Budget: 95px. The longest title word, navigation, is 78px at 13px/600.
  • Narrow mode is a different grid template on the same markup: when the cards’ container is under 380px (viewport < ~470px), each card becomes a full-width row: thumbnail 80px on the left, the three text lines on the right (≈190px of text at 360). Same rows, same budget, no separate CSS for text.

Test plan

One card matrix test runs every combination of viewport {360, 600, 800, 1011, 1012, 1280, 1500} × theme {light, dark} × font {GitHub’s stack, "DejaVu Sans" as the wide worst case}, with GitHub’s label rule in the fixture. A failure message prints the card rect, each child’s rect, the computed font and the panel width.

Risks and open questions

  • The 440px panel covers more of the list at 1012–1100px. Default: accept it; the panel is a popover, it closes on Esc or an outside click, and the mockup is wider.
  • The descriptions change from the mockup’s wording (“Horizontal filters”, “Collapsible groups”) to shorter ones. Default: shorter copy; the issue calls them indicative and requires a short description.
  • :has() needs Chrome 105+. Default: fine for an MV3 extension; without it the radio still shows Chrome’s own focus ring.

Out of scope

  • Everything outside the layout cards (button, anchors, preferences, state matrix): unchanged.
  • Rendering the three layouts (#106) and the reasonWhy split (#104).
